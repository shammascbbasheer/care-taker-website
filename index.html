            What services can I enquire about?
            

              Home and office deep cleaning, sanitization, bathroom and interior
              cleaning, local and commercial moving, packing, unpacking,
              crating, long-distance moving, and furniture assembly.
            

          

          

            Where are the services available?
            

              The posters advertise services in Kochi. Please call to confirm
              coverage for your locality or moving route.
            

          

          

            How much will my service cost?
            

              All listed services are ₹2,000–₹3,999 except water tank cleaning,
              which is ₹999. Confirm the scope and any additional charges with
              Caretaker before booking.
            

          

          

            Does this form confirm a booking?
            

              No. It opens WhatsApp with your enquiry. You must tap Send in
              WhatsApp for Caretaker to receive it. A booking is confirmed only
              after the business agrees to the price, availability, and service
              date.
            

          

          

            Can I request both cleaning and moving?
            

              Both are advertised. Ask the team whether the combination you need
              can be arranged for your location and schedule.
